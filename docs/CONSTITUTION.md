# Company Constitution

The canonical machine-readable rules live in `src/constitution.ts`. They require lawful mission alignment, authoritative current truth, traceable and preferably reversible actions, delegated authority, human approval boundaries, privacy, independent audit, evidence-based hiring, convergence, and immutable safeguards/audit history.

Constitutional changes should be versioned decisions approved by the company owner and reviewed by Internal Audit. Agents cannot amend their own authority.
